Mobility & Restoration Lead

Clara Chen

RYT-500 Certified Yoga Instructor // Athletic Mobility Specialist

Clara bridges the gap between mindful movement and athletic recovery. She works with lifters, runners, and everyday professionals to improve joint range of motion, release deep muscular tension, and reset central nervous system fatigue.

Licensed Zumba & Strong Nation Instructor // Certified Group Fitness Coach
