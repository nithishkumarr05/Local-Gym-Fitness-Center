The Culture

The Code
Of Conduct

To preserve a safe, clean, and motivating environment for all members, everyone agrees to uphold these foundational standards of mutual respect and focus.

01

Re-Rack & Wipe Down

Always return dumbbells, barbell plates, and accessories to their designated racks after your set. Wipe down all equipment with provided sanitizing wipes.

02

Mindful Training Floor

Take phone calls in the lounge or reception area. To respect member privacy and traffic flow, tripods and obstructive video recording setups are not permitted on the gym floor.

03

Athletic Attire & Footwear

Clean athletic activewear and closed-toe sports shoes are required across all training areas to maintain maximum floor safety and hygiene standards.

04

Shared Space Courtesy

Please avoid dropping weights outside Olympic lifting platforms with bumper plates. During busy training windows, kindly allow fellow members to work in between sets.

Safety-First Protocol
Health & Safety Standards

Your Health & Safety Protocols

Effective training starts with a safe, injury-free foundation. We maintain strict protocols across equipment inspection, lifting safety, and floor hygiene.

Form & Barbell Collars

Prioritize controlled movement mechanics before increasing load. Always secure barbell collars and warm up with dynamic mobility drills.

Spotters & Safety Catches

Always set safety pins in squat racks, and never hesitate to ask our coaches or fellow members for a spot on heavy bench presses or compound lifts.

Club Cleanliness & Hygiene

Hospital-grade sanitizing wipe stations and touchless hand sanitizers are situated throughout the club. Please wipe grips and pads after each set.

Prompt Hazard Reporting

Our staff conducts continuous equipment inspections. If you notice a loose bolt, worn cable, or floor spill, please inform our team immediately.
